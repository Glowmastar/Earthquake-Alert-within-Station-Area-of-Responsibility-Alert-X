# Earthquake Alert within Station Area of Responsibility

## Project Lead

Glyn Bryan Inguito Ebardo

## Section / Unit

STNO

## Workshop

AI Coding Workshop Batch 2

## Purpose

Develop a Google Sheets and Google Apps Script prototype that helps
authorized station and Data Receiving Center personnel identify earthquake
information relevant to a defined Station Area of Responsibility.

The prototype will organize earthquake information according to approved
province/municipality AOR mappings and send email notifications to responsible
personnel when an event is identified within their respective AOR.

## Problem

Earthquake information is not currently easy for station personnel to sort
according to geographic Area of Responsibility. This can make it slower to
identify information relevant to:

- Preliminary Quake Reports.
- Aftershock-count monitoring.
- Station-level earthquake monitoring.

## Initial Prototype

The intended workshop prototype uses:

- Google Sheets.
- Google Apps Script.
- An existing earthquake-information website or source.
- Approved province/municipality AOR mappings.
- Email notifications.

## Intended Users

Relevant users include:

- Station Officers or station personnel.
- Data Receiving Center personnel.
- Other authorized PHIVOLCS personnel.

Final user groups, accounts, and permissions remain to be validated.

## Initial Workshop Scope

The participant's minimum prototype target is:

- Three selected provinces.
- All municipalities within those selected provinces.

The specific provinces and official AOR assignments are still to be provided.
