# Initial Google Sheets Prototype Plan

The prototype GUI is the Google Sheets workbook itself. The workbook should
remain simple and intuitive, prioritizing fast reading, minimal manual
sorting, clear geographic context, and simple operation during duty.

## Recommended Initial Worksheet Concept

### 1. AOR VIEW

Primary user-facing worksheet. Its purpose is to show earthquake information
relevant to a selected or assigned AOR.

### 2. EARTHQUAKE DATA

Raw or normalized earthquake information retrieved from the approved source.

### 3. AOR MAPPING

Approved station → province → municipality mapping.

### 4. ALERT LOG

Records of alert-processing results.

### 5. CONFIGURATION

Only if required after source and operational requirements are reviewed.

## Deferred Sheet Details

Exact columns, formulas, source mappings, recipients, and workflow behavior
must not be finalized until the actual earthquake-information source and AOR
data are analyzed.

Do not add unnecessary dashboards or decorative complexity during the initial
prototype phase.
